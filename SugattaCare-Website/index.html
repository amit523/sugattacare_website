Who we are

Sugatta Care Foundation is a registered, non-profit social welfare organisation. We began with a simple idea: help the poor, the elderly and the needy, with no personal profit motive.

We believe a strong society is built on kindness, equal chances and help that arrives on time.

Our vision and mission

Vision: A world where every person has basic healthcare, a life of dignity, and a safe place to grow.

Mission: Spread health awareness. Give care and comfort to people in need. Teach women and youth skills for work. Develop villages and protect nature. Stay open about every rupee.

Questions people ask

Will I get a tax receipt?

Eligible donors can get an 80G receipt. After you give, write to us with your name, PAN and proof of payment.

Where does my money go?

All of it goes to our charity work: health camps, care for elders, learning for children and village projects. No one takes profit.

How do I donate safely?

Ask us for our bank or UPI details by email, and check them with us before you send money. We never ask for your bank password or OTP.

Can I volunteer?

Yes. Doctors, nurses, teachers and anyone with a kind heart are welcome. Use the contact form and choose Volunteer.

Can I give clothes, books or medicines?

Clothes, books and health kits are welcome. Please talk to us before sending medicines. We only give medicines under a qualified doctor.

How do you use my details?

We use your name and phone or email only to reply to you. We do not sell or share them. We share a photo only with the person’s permission. To remove a photo or your details, write to us.
