Our 100% honesty promise.

Because we are legally registered as a Section 8 company, we cannot and do not distribute profits to any director or member. Every donation we receive is used for our charitable programs and the people we serve.

We measure our growth not in bank balances, but in the smiles we bring back to struggling faces.

Our services

We work on the ground, in villages and towns, with practical support in four areas where local effort can make an immediate difference.

Health & preventive medical awareness

We organise local health awareness camps in villages and semi-urban areas, teaching families simple habits around cleanliness, nutrition and early disease prevention.

Senior citizen & child welfare

We support senior care initiatives and old-age homes, while providing nutritious food, clothing and daily essentials to children from struggling families.

Education & livelihood skills

We distribute books and learning kits so children stay in school, and run practical skill-building workshops for women and young people.

Rural development & environmental care

We work alongside villagers through tree-plantation drives, cleaner surroundings and timely emergency relief during local crises.

Who we are

We may be a startup NGO, but our purpose is clear and honest.

Starting from our roots in Jind, Haryana, we work directly with families, children and elderly citizens to bring basic healthcare awareness, education and everyday dignity to overlooked communities. No big corporate layers, no wasted funds—just real people helping real people.

Our story & legal roots

Sugatta Care Foundation was officially incorporated on 30 July 2025 under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013. Our registered office is in Jind, Haryana – 126113, and our CIN is U86909HR2025NPL134592.

We started with an honest belief that you do not need to wait to become a large organisation before caring for your neighbours. We stay close to the ground, listen to what families need, and provide practical, timely support.

How we handle every rupee: Every donation is recorded, independently audited and used strictly for charitable programs. Our founders and members do not take profit from the foundation.

Our mission & vision

We dream of a society where no child skips school because of poverty, no elder feels abandoned, and no family suffers from preventable illness because nobody guided them in time.

Prevent before cure

Take basic health, hygiene and nutrition awareness directly to rural households before small issues become major illnesses.

Care with dignity

Stand beside neglected senior citizens and underprivileged children with food, essential support and genuine emotional warmth.

Teach skills that pay

Help women and young people learn practical skills so they can earn with pride and support their families.

Protect villages and nature

Keep rural communities clean, green and resilient through plantation drives and timely help during emergencies.

Questions people ask

Will I get a tax receipt?

Eligible donors can get an 80G receipt. After you give, write to us with your name, PAN and proof of payment.

Where does my money go?

All of it goes to our charity work: health camps, care for elders, learning for children and village projects. No one takes profit.

How do I donate safely?

Ask us for our bank or UPI details by email, and check them with us before you send money. We never ask for your bank password or OTP.

Can I volunteer?

Yes. Doctors, nurses, teachers and anyone with a kind heart are welcome. Use the contact form and choose Volunteer.

Can I give clothes, books or medicines?

Clothes, books and health kits are welcome. Please talk to us before sending medicines. We only give medicines under a qualified doctor.

How do you use my details?

We use your name and phone or email only to reply to you. We do not sell or share them. We share a photo only with the person’s permission. To remove a photo or your details, write to us.
